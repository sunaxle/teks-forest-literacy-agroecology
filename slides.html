Phase 2: The Biological Mechanism

Trees are Living Solar Water Pumps

Thermodynamics & Stomata
💧 1. Deep Root Pumping
Roots drink hundreds of gallons of cold water deep beneath South Texas soils.
🔬 2. Microscopic Stomata
Leaves exhale pure water vapor through millions of microscopic mouth-like pores.
❄️ 3. Latent Heat Absorption
When liquid water evaporates into gas, it sucks heat directly out of the school air!
🌳 ➔ 💧 ➔ 🌬️
"Active Evaporative Cooling"

Just like humans sweat to cool off during soccer practice, trees sweat to cool the entire schoolyard!

Phase 3: The Math Multiplier

Becoming a Human Measuring Tape

TEKS Math 5.4H ($A \approx \pi r^2$)
🚶‍♂️ Step 1: Calibrate Your Pace
Walk 10 normal paces along our 10m tape measure. 10 paces = 10 meters! (1 pace = 1.0 m).
📐 Step 2: Measure Radius ($r$)
Pace from tree trunk to shadow edge in 4 directions: North, South, East, West.
🛡️ Step 3: Compute Recess Refuge
$\text{Area} = 3.14 \times r^2$. Divide by $1.5\,\text{m}^2$ to find how many students fit in the shade!
CANOPY SHADOW FORMULA:
$\text{Area} = 3.14 \times r^2$
Example: Radius $r = 3\,\text{m}$ ➔ $\text{Area} \approx 28.3\,\text{m}^2$ ➔ 18 Students Protected!

"We aren't just doing science today—we are surveying engineers calculating our campus thermal safety capacity."
