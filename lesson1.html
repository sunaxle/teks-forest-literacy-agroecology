Urban Microclimate & Transpiration Audit

Empirical Thermodynamics, Pediatric Heat Safety & Evaporative Canopy Cooling
🎯 Grade Band: Grades 3–5 (Elementary) ⏱️ Duration: 45–90 Min (1–2 Outdoor Sessions) 📐 Core Model: Dual-Unit ($mL/hr$ & $A \approx \pi r^2$)
Anchor Phenomenon & Central Field Mystery
"On a 95°F afternoon in South Texas, why does an asphalt basketball court heat up to over 135°F—hot enough to cause pediatric skin burns—while the soil beneath a mature Honey Mesquite tree stays a cool 85°F? And why does a dry plastic bag sealed over a leafy tree branch fill with pure, clean liquid water drops in under an hour?"

TEACHER 5E GUIDE

5E Instructional Blueprint

Turn-Key 45–90 Minute Outdoor Field Progression
Phase 1: Engage
The Asphalt Frying Pan Hook 15 Min
Gather at the edge of the schoolyard asphalt. Touch test analogy: "Why does canvas shade feel hot and stuffy, but tree shade feels like an active AC?" Introduce the living solar pump mechanism. Seal a 1-gallon clear Ziploc bag over 20–30 sunlit leaves on a native Mesquite or Elm branch with a zip-tie.
Phase 2: Explore
6-Zone IR Heat Transect & Pacing 30 Min
Calibrate 10-step pace along a 10m tape measure. Measure tree canopy shadow radius in 4 directions. Using non-contact IR guns, record 3 repeated surface temperatures across 6 zones (Asphalt, Turf, Open Grass, Tree Shade, Trunk Bark, Canopy Leaves).
Phase 3: Explain
Stomata Harvest & Latent Heat 20 Min
Harvest the condensation bag. Pour water into a 10 mL graduated cylinder. Inspect microscopic stomata on leaf undersides using 10x hand lenses. Explain latent heat of vaporization: phase change absorbs thermal energy directly from schoolyard air.
Phase 4 & 5: Elaborate & Evaluate
Thermal Refuge Capacity & CER Synthesis 25 Min
Compute total shade area ($A = \pi r^2$) and recess student capacity ($1.5 \text{m}^2/\text{child}$). Students write an evidence-based Claim-Evidence-Reasoning (CER) report citing exact temperature drop and transpiration milliliters.
🎒 Team Field Kit (Per 3–4 Students):
• 1 Infrared (IR) Thermometer Gun · • 1 Clear 1-Gal Ziploc + Zip-Tie · • 1 Graduated Cylinder (10 mL) · • 1 Hand Lens (10x) · • 1 Metric Tape (10m) · • 1 Field Clipboard + Worksheet.

🌐 Sombra Sana Dual-Language Academic Word Bank:
• Transpiración / Transpiration: Evaporative loss of water vapor through leaf stomata.
• Estomas / Stomata: Microscopic leaf pores regulating gas exchange and moisture.
• Microclima / Microclimate: Local atmospheric zone differing from surrounding area.
• Sombra Moteada / Dappled Shade: Filtered sunlight beneath living native canopy.
