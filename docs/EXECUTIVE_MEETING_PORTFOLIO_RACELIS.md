### A. The $4,950 "Sweet Spot" Campus Architecture
Under **Texas Education Code § 44.031(j)**, school district expenditures under **$50,000 aggregate** (or micro-purchases under $10,000 for individual campus discretionary funds) can be procured via **sole-source direct purchase without requiring complex competitive sealed bidding (RFP)**.

Each $4,950 Campus Starter Pack includes:
1. **Full Digital Platform License:** Unlimited teacher & student logins to the SombraTEKS Outdoor Lab Hub, DAG Knowledge Graph, and Interactive Rubrics.
2. **52-Module Physical Classroom Curriculum Box:** Laminated, weatherproof teacher guides and print-ready master student clipboard kits.
3. **Turn-Key Field Sensor Starter Kit:**
   - Dual-laser non-contact infrared thermal gun
   - Digital soil moisture probe & penetrometer
   - Biltmore stick forestry measuring kit & clinometers
   - Digital handheld weather psychrometer (ambient temp & relative humidity)
4. **4 Hours of Accredited Teacher Professional Development (CPE Hours):** Certified training by UTRGV Agroecology educational facilitators.

### B. District-Wide Valuation: Donna ISD Case Study
- **Campuses:** 22 Total (13 Elementary, 4 Middle, 3 High Schools, 2 Alternative/Specialized)
- **Annual Contract Value:** $108,900 / year ($4,950 $\times$ 22)
- **District Net Cost:** **$0.00 from local maintenance & operations (M&O) tax funds.**

### C. Federal Grant & Categorical Funding Streams (100% Reimbursable)
School districts will fund 100% of SombraTEKS adoption through existing federal and state entitlement grants:
- **Title II, Part A (Supporting Effective Instruction):** Covers teacher training, STEM pedagogical coaching, and interdisciplinary curriculum materials.
- **Title IV, Part A (Student Support and Academic Enrichment - SSAE):** Mandates funding for well-rounded education, hands-on STEM programs, and environmental health education.
- **USDA Forest Service / Texas A&M Forest Service Urban Forestry Grants:** Dedicated regional tree canopy and environmental education funds.

### D. Region One ESC Addressable Market: $1.98 Million
- Region One ESC serves **38 public school districts and 8 charter networks** across Cameron, Hidalgo, Starr, Willacy, Zapata, and Webb counties.
- Total campus universe: **~400 elementary, middle, and high school campuses**.
- Total addressable curriculum licensing market: **$1,980,000**.
