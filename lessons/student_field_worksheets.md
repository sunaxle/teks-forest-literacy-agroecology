## Flagship Module 1: The Urban Microclimate & Thermal Transpiration Audit (Grades 3–5)
*Standards: Science 5.12B, Math 5.4H, Health 5.3A, PLT Theme 1 & 2*  
*Full Teacher Guide & Rubric:* [`MODULE_1_URBAN_MICROCLIMATE_TRANSPIRATION_AUDIT.md`](MODULE_1_URBAN_MICROCLIMATE_TRANSPIRATION_AUDIT.md)

```text
================================================================================
                    CAMPUS MICROFOREST FIELD INVESTIGATION
        MODULE 1: URBAN MICROCLIMATE & THERMAL TRANSPIRATION AUDIT
================================================================================
Field Investigator Desk/Team ID: ____________   Date: __________________________
Partner Codename: ____________________________  Class Period: __________________
Tree Tag ID / Species: ________________________  Weather / Sun Conditions: _____

[ PART 1: FIELD HYPOTHESIS ]
Circle your prediction before taking outdoor measurements:
1. The difference between unshaded asphalt and tree shade will be:
   (A) Less than 10°F / 5.5°C   (B) 10°F to 25°F   (C) Greater than 30°F / 16.7°C
2. The sealed branch bag will collect liquid water because leaves:
   (A) Absorb water from air (B) Transpire water vapor (C) Melt in the sun

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[ PART 2: STEP PACE CALIBRATION (Math 5.4H) ]
Walk 10 normal walking steps along the 10-meter (32.8 ft) tape measure.
- Total distance walked for 10 steps = ___________ meters (m) / ___________ feet (ft)
- My Personal Step Pace = (Distance ÷ 10) = ___________ m/step  (___________ ft/step)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[ PART 3: CANOPY SHADOW RADIUS & COOLING AREA CALCULATION ]
Pace from the center of the tree trunk to the outer edge of the shadow:
- North Radius (r1): _____ steps × (Pace: _____ m) = _________ meters (_____ ft)
- South Radius (r2): _____ steps × (Pace: _____ m) = _________ meters (_____ ft)
- East Radius  (r3): _____ steps × (Pace: _____ m) = _________ meters (_____ ft)
- West Radius  (r4): _____ steps × (Pace: _____ m) = _________ meters (_____ ft)

Average Radius (r_avg) = (r1 + r2 + r3 + r4) ÷ 4 = ____________ meters (_____ ft)

[ AREA COMPUTATION WORKSPACE ]
Formula for Shaded Circle Area: Area = 3.14 × (r_avg)²
1. (r_avg)² = (_________ m) × (_________ m) = _________ m² (_________ sq ft)
2. Total Shade Area = 3.14 × (_________ m²) = ________________ square meters (m²)

Thermal Refuge Capacity: If each student needs 1.5 m² (16.1 sq ft) of shade:
Total Students Shaded = (Total Area _________ m²) ÷ 1.5 = _________ students!

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[ PART 4: 6-ZONE INFRARED SURFACE TEMPERATURE AUDIT (Science 5.12B / 5.1C) ]
Aim the IR thermometer gun perpendicular 12 inches (30 cm) from surface:

+-------------------------------------+---------+---------+---------+-----------+
| Microclimate Surface Zone           | Trial 1 | Trial 2 | Trial 3 | Average   |
+-------------------------------------+---------+---------+---------+-----------+
| 1. Unshaded Blacktop Asphalt        |  ____°F |  ____°F |  ____°F |  _______°F|
| 2. Unshaded Synthetic Playground    |  ____°F |  ____°F |  ____°F |  _______°F|
| 3. Unshaded Lawn Grass (Full Sun)   |  ____°F |  ____°F |  ____°F |  _______°F|
| 4. Shaded Grass / Mulch Under Tree  |  ____°F |  ____°F |  ____°F |  _______°F|
| 5. Living Tree Trunk Bark           |  ____°F |  ____°F |  ____°F |  _______°F|
| 6. Tree Canopy Leaves (Underside)   |  ____°F |  ____°F |  ____°F |  _______°F|
+-------------------------------------+---------+---------+---------+-----------+

Thermal Delta: Unshaded Asphalt (____°F / ____°C) - Tree Shade (____°F / ____°C) = ____°F COOLER!

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[ PART 5: TRANSPIRATION HARVEST & STOMATA AUDIT ]
- Volume of Liquid Water Transpired in 45 min: [ _________ ] mL  (_________ fl oz)
- Stomata Count observed under 10x hand lens:  [ _________ ] pores per field
================================================================================
```

```text
[ SECTION 1: CLINOMETER FORESTRY TRIGONOMETRY (Math 8.7B / Sci 8.1A) ]
             
             /|  <-- Sighted Tree Apex (Angle: θ)
            / |
           /  | Tree Height (H) = D · tan(θ) + h_eye
          /   |
         /θ___|
     Eye |    |
   Height|____| Tree Base
         |<--- Baseline Distance (D) --->|

[ TRIGONOMETRIC COMPUTATION WORKSPACE ]

- Step A: (D _________ m) × tan(θ: _______) = _________ meters

Measure the longest shadow cast on the ground from the tree base to the shadow tip:
- Ground Shadow Length (L_shadow): ____________ meters (_____ ft)
- Calculate Solar Elevation Angle: tan(θ_solar) = (H_tree _________ m) ÷ (L_shadow _________ m)
- tan(θ_solar) = _____________ --> Sighted Solar Elevation Angle = ___________ degrees (°)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Formula: Albedo (α) = Reflected Light (S_out) ÷ Incident Light (S_in)
Net Solar Absorbed = 1000 W/m² × (1 - α)

|                            | (Lux Up) | (Lux Down)| (S_out/S_in| Energy (W/m²) | Temp (°F) |
+----------------------------+----------+-----------+------------+---------------+-----------+
| 1. Fresh Blacktop Asphalt  |  ______  |  ______   |   0._____  |  _______ W/m² |  _______°F|
| 2. Light Concrete Sidewalk |  ______  |  ______   |   0._____  |  _______ W/m² |  _______°F|
| 3. Unshaded Turf Grass     |  ______  |  ______   |   0._____  |  _______ W/m² |  _______°F|
| 4. Tree Canopy / Leaf Bed  |  ______  |  ______   |   0._____  |  _______ W/m² |  _______°F|
+----------------------------+----------+-----------+------------+---------------+-----------+

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[ SECTION 4: HISTORICAL GEOGRAPHY & URBAN MODIFICATION (SS 8.11A) ]
```
