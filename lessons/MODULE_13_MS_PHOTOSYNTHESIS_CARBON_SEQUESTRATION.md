## II. Master Teacher Instructional Guide (5E Cycle)

### 1. Essential Question & Phenomenon
> **Anchor Phenomenon:** A mature 40-year-old Live Oak or Texas Ebony on our school campus weighs over $2{,}500\text{ kilograms}$ ($5{,}500\text{ pounds}$)—the weight of two pickup trucks! Yet decades ago, it started as a tiny seed weighing only a single gram. Where did all those thousands of kilograms of heavy, solid wood come from? It didn't come from the soil dirt—the ground didn't cave in! It was pulled right out of thin air by photosynthesis. How much invisible atmospheric $CO_2$ gas is locked inside our campus trees right now?
> 
> **Essential Question:** *How do campus trees convert atmospheric carbon dioxide into solid dry biomass, and how can we quantify their total sequestered carbon vault?*

---

### 2. Learning Objectives & Success Criteria
- **Science Objective:** Students will balance the chemical equation for oxygenic photosynthesis, identify the stomatal gas-exchange mechanism ($CO_2$ in, $O_2 + H_2O$ out), and explain that $50\%$ of dry wood mass is elemental solid carbon.
- **Math Objective:** Students will measure trunk circumference at Breast Height ($4.5\text{ ft} / 1.37\text{ m}$), compute trunk DBH ($\text{DBH} = C / \pi$), estimate total dry biomass ($M_{\text{dry}}$), and calculate total sequestered carbon mass ($M_C = 0.50 \times M_{\text{dry}}$) and equivalent atmospheric $CO_2$ ($M_{CO2} = M_C \times 3.67$) in dual metric and imperial units ($\text{kg / lbs}$).
- **Social Studies/Health Objective:** Students will calculate the annual carbon offset equivalent in vehicle passenger miles and monetize the schoolyard ecosystem service value.

### 5. 5E Instructional Learning Sequence

#### Phase 1: ENGAGE (10 Minutes) — The "Trees are Made of Air" Hook
1. **The Mass Source Mystery:** Gather students in front of the largest campus tree:
   - *"If I burn a log, it turns into smoke and invisible gas. When a tree grows, it does the exact reverse! A tree is solid air, woven by sunlight and water. Every wooden desk, branch, and leaf in this school was captured from atmospheric carbon dioxide gas ($CO_2$)."*
2. **Biochemical Equation Anchor (Science 8.12A):**
   $$6\text{CO}_2 \text{ (gas)} + 6\text{H}_2\text{O} \text{ (liquid)} + \text{Photons} \longrightarrow \text{C}_6\text{H}_{12}\text{O}_6 \text{ (glucose)} + 6\text{O}_2 \text{ (gas)}$$
3. **Walk to Microforest Stations:** Assign teams to tagged study trees.

#### Phase 2 & 3: EXPLORE & EXPLAIN (25 Minutes) — 3 Rotational Field Stations

##### Station 1: Tree DBH Forestry Survey & Dry Biomass (`STA-DBH-ALLOMETRY-SURVEY` · 8 Min)
- **Forestry Measurement Protocol (Math 8.7B):**
  - Locate Standard Breast Height: exactly $1.37\text{ meters}$ ($4.5\text{ feet}$) above ground level.
  - Wrap measuring tape flat around the trunk to find Circumference ($C$): _______ $\text{cm}$ / _______ $\text{inches}$.
  - Compute Diameter at Breast Height (DBH):
    $$\text{DBH} = \frac{\text{Circumference}}{\pi} = \frac{C}{3.1416} = \text{_______ cm (_______ inches)}$$
- **Allometric Dry Biomass Lookup (USDA Forest Service Model):**
  - Using the species allometric formula ($M_{\text{dry}} = a \cdot \text{DBH}^b$) or reference chart:
    - *Example (Honey Mesquite / Live Oak):* If $\text{DBH} = 25\text{ cm}$ ($9.8\text{ in}$), estimated Aboveground Dry Biomass ($M_{\text{dry}}$) $\approx 220\text{ kg}$ ($485\text{ lbs}$).
    - Team Study Tree Dry Biomass ($M_{\text{dry}}$) = _______ $\text{kg}$ (_______ $\text{lbs}$).

##### Station 2: Solid Carbon Mass & CO2 Gas Vault Calculator (`STA-CARBON-VAULT-COMPUTATION` · 8 Min)
- **Step A: Elemental Solid Carbon Mass ($M_C$):**
  - Dry wood is approximately $50\%$ pure elemental carbon by mass (the rest is oxygen and hydrogen in cellulose/lignin).
  $$M_C = 0.50 \times M_{\text{dry}} = 0.50 \times \text{_______ kg} = \text{_______ kg of solid carbon (_______ lbs)}$$
- **Step B: Equivalent Atmospheric $CO_2$ Sequestered ($M_{CO2}$):**
  - Carbon dioxide has a molecular weight of $44\text{ g/mol}$ (Carbon = $12$, Oxygen = $16 \times 2 = 32$). The conversion factor is $44 \div 12 \approx 3.67$.
  $$M_{CO2} = M_C \times 3.67 = \text{_______ kg } C \times 3.67 = \text{_______ kg of } CO_2 \text{ gas locked away! (_______ lbs)}$$

##### Station 3: Campus Whole-Grove Carbon Offset & Economic Value (`STA-GROVE-OFFSET-VALUATION` · 8 Min)
- **Passenger Vehicle Emissions Equivalent (Social Studies 8.11A):**
  - Burning 1 gallon of gasoline emits approximately $8.89\text{ kg}$ ($19.6\text{ lbs}$) of $CO_2$.
  - Gallons of Gas Offset = $(\text{Total } CO_2 \text{ Sequestered: _______ kg}) \div 8.89\text{ kg/gal} = \text{_______ gallons of gasoline!}$
  - Driving Miles Offset ($25\text{ mpg}$ avg) = $\text{Gallons} \times 25\text{ miles} = \text{_______ miles of driving offset!}$
- **Whole Campus Microforest Vault:**
  - If our campus microforest has 30 mature native trees:
  $$\text{Total Campus Carbon Vault} = 30 \times M_{CO2} = \text{_______ kg } CO_2 \text{ (_______ metric tons)}$$
- **Economic Value:** At $\$50.00$ per metric ton on the carbon credit market, our campus forest carbon value = $\$ \text{_______}$.

#### Phase 4: ELABORATE (5 Minutes) — Urban Forestry Climate Resilience
- Connect the findings to regional municipal forestry:
  - *"How does transforming a bare turf schoolyard into a 50-tree microforest fight both regional climate change and localized urban heat?"*

#### Phase 5: EVALUATE (5 Minutes) — Carbon CER Report & Formative Wrap-Up
- Students synthesize their mathematical calculations into a formal Claim-Evidence-Reasoning carbon audit summary.
- Return tools and line up for classroom return.

## III. Misconception Alerts & Differentiation Scaffolds

- ❌ *Misconception:* "Trees get their structural building mass from soil fertilizer and minerals."  
  ✔️ *Correction:* Soil minerals account for less than 1% of tree mass. Over 99% of dry wood biomass is carbon, hydrogen, and oxygen synthesized directly from $CO_2$ gas and water via sunlight.
- ❌ *Misconception:* "A dead fallen tree holds carbon forever."  
  ✔️ *Correction:* When dead wood rots or burns, decomposers and combustion release the stored carbon back into the atmosphere as $CO_2$ gas.
- 💡 *Scaffolding for Mathematics:* Provide pre-programmed formulas and clear step-by-step boxed calculation workflows with dual-unit multipliers.
- 🚀 *Advanced Extension (GT):* Derive the logarithmic power-law biomass regression: $\ln(M) = -2.13 + 2.45 \cdot \ln(\text{DBH})$ and graph on semi-log scale.
